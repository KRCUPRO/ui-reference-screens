IV. Asynchronous Synthesis

Rather than relying on immediate responses, Marginalia allows concepts to coalesce over time. Ideas entered into the system are not aggressively categorized, but permitted to loosely associate based on semantic proximity, fostering an environment where novel insights emerge organically from the synthesis of seemingly disparate notes.

V. Intentional Degradation

Acknowledging the overwhelming nature of infinite retention, notes that are infrequently accessed or lack structural linkage slowly fade into the archive. This process of intentional degradation mimics human memory, prioritizing active, relevant thought patterns while clearing mental space, ensuring the canvas remains focused on current intellectual pursuits.
