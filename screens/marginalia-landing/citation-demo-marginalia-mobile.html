What was our churn rate in Q3?

Based on the retention analysis, the overall user churn rate for the third quarter stabilized at 2.4%[1]. This represents a slight improvement compared to the preceding quarter, primarily attributed to the rollout of the new onboarding sequence.

However, segmenting by cohort reveals that free-tier users experienced a marginally higher attrition rate, underscoring the need for continued optimization in top-of-funnel engagement strategies.
