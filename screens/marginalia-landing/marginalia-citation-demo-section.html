Query
What was our churn rate in Q3?

Our analysis indicates a significant shift in user retention during the third quarter. The overall customer churn rate for Q3 settled at 4.2%[1]. This represents a marginal improvement from the previous quarter, largely attributed to the implementation of the new automated onboarding sequence.

However, when segmenting the data by user cohort, enterprise clients exhibited a notably lower churn rate of just 1.5%[2] over the same period, suggesting that high-touch account management strategies remain highly effective.
