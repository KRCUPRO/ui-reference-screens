Data retention

Marginalia enforces strict data retention schedules to ensure research materials are preserved only as long as necessary for academic utility and compliance. Our retention architecture is designed to balance the persistent needs of scholarly archives with fundamental data minimization principles.

Upon the termination of an academic subscription or individual researcher account, an automated cryptographic erasure process is initiated. This process ensures that all primary source documents, annotations, and generated bibliographies are permanently purged from active storage tiers within a defined grace period, preventing residual data exposure.

Certain telemetry and audit logs necessary for security forensics and systemic integrity monitoring are retained on a segregated, immutable ledger. These logs undergo aggressive data masking to obfuscate personally identifiable information (PII) before entering the long-term archiving phase, aligning with international privacy frameworks.
